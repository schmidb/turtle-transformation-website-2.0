# Brand and content rules

## Offerings
- **Primary offer:** the 12-month executive coaching with Miriam Schmidberger. Everything on the home page leads to it.
- **Secondary offer:** the advisory practice. Its public name is always **"AI & Data Advisory"** (AI first, because AI is the priority). Never write "Data & AI Advisory", "Data and AI" or similar in visible text, metadata, schema, FAQ or `llms.txt`.
- The route `/data-ai-consulting/` is kept for SEO. Do not rename it without a redirect plan.
- No German version of the site.

## Programme facts (authoritative: the supplied programme overview)
- Duration 12 months. Each month: one 60-minute coaching session plus one 30-minute practical application/training session.
- Investment EUR 5,400, payable in two installments of EUR 2,700, with a progress review after six months.
- Optional between-session support (asynchronous messaging, Tuesday and Thursday): EUR 280 per month.
- Individual 60-minute sessions outside the programme: EUR 450.
- Baseline assessments: Adaptability Assessment and Five-Mountain Assessment.
- Delivery: hybrid, online or in person.
- If any of these change, update page copy, FAQ, JSON-LD offers, `public/llms.txt` and `docs/` together.

## Conversion
- Contact is by email only: `info@turtletrafo.com`. There is no application form and no qualification criteria.
- The primary CTA is "Request a confidential conversation" with a response promise of within 48 hours.
- Show the email once per CTA area. Do not repeat the address directly below a button that already shows it.

## Testimonials and proof
- Use only approved testimonials: Alexandra, Anna, Thorsten (also written Torsten Niedermeyer in source material) and the Ali and Thorsten YouTube videos.
- Never invent surnames, job titles, employers, metrics or quotes. Do not use health or diagnostic details.
- Never use the Rachel O'Sullivan recording or any unapproved or workshop-level testimonial as proof for the 12-month programme.
- Ask the owner before adding any new testimonial.

## Tone
Specific and credible, not generic coaching language. Avoid unsupported outcome claims.

## Domain
- The only public domain is `https://turtletrafo.com`. `turtletrafo.de` redirects to it. Do not publish `.de` addresses.
- `old.turtletrafo.com` is the legacy GoHighLevel site (noindex). Do not link to it.
